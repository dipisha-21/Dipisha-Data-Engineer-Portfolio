description: Why the proof-led visual direction was selected for the Data Engineer portfolio.
---

Use the systems proof-led design direction as the basis for the public portfolio. Keep the editorial narrative as an exploration reference, not the primary site.

**Why:** The requested 60-second recruiter scan benefits more from immediate role alignment, quantified ETL evidence, and contact actions than from a career-story-first opening. The source resume does not document formal people management, so show verified engineering ownership without claiming leadership positions.

**How to apply:** When turning the visual mockup into a working site or extending it, preserve a fast first-screen scan and evidence-linked deeper review. Keep claims tied to the supplied resume and omit private contact details.
